Set up on Monday, publish by lunch

Rosterly takes your team from a blank calendar to a published rota in three steps.

  1. Add your team

    Import staff from a spreadsheet or type them in. Set roles, hourly rates and availability once.

  2. Build the week

    Drag shifts onto the calendar. Open slots can be offered to anyone who is free.

  3. Publish and relax

    Your team gets a notification with their shifts. Swaps and time-off requests come to you for approval.
